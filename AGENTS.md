# Project standards

- Lumen Breaker is an original autonomous brick-breaker screensaver. Keep release claims tied to recorded evidence.
- Keep com.jeremykenedy.lumenbreaker and its own external signing identity.
- Keep runtime offline with no requested permissions, SDKs, network or reporting.
- Keep physics and options Android-free and behaviorally tested.
- Render at actual View dimensions; stop callbacks on pause and detach.
- No gameplay words, HUD, logos or watermarks.
- Require native moving visual, settings, lifecycle, Dream and physical pacing gates before release.
- Preserve all other screensaver projects and frozen candidates.
